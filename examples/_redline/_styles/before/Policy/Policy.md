## Scope

This policy applies to all laptops of the team. It covers how devices are handed out, used and returned.

## Rules

- Lock the screen when you leave the desk.
- Install updates once a week.
- Use the password manager for all accounts.
- Store work files on the shared drive.
- Report a lost device within one day.

## Devices

| Device | Owner      | Review |
| ------ | ---------- | ------ |
| Laptop | IT team    | yearly |
| Phone  | Each user  | yearly |

## Contact

Questions go to the service desk. The service desk answers within one working day.

## Exceptions

Test devices are exempt from the update rule.
